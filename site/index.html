The gear above is its equations

Section B · Parameters

Change the module, the teeth follow

The spur gear sample is driven by a handful of global variables. Its tooth gap, pitch, hub and lightening holes are all expressions of them, so editing z from 36 to 24 rebuilds the whole part.

Conditions can suppress features, and design tables give rows of sizes, so one part file can be a whole family of parts.
